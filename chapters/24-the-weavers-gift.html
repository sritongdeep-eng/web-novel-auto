    CHAPTER 24 — The Weavers’ Gift

    The Weavers’ message arrived in the static like a sunrise.

    Kael stood in the Blood Network’s control room, his threads extended into the infinite, when the signal hit — not as data, not as code, but as understanding. The Weavers were speaking directly to his consciousness, bypassing language, bypassing technology, bypassing everything that separated minds.

    The Experiment is complete, they said. Humanity has chosen preservation over destruction. You have earned the next step.

    “What next step?” Kael asked.

    The Weavers showed him visions — of Earth eighty years from now, of cities rebuilt on alien worlds, of consciousness traveling through the static like thoughts through a global mind. They showed him the Seed — a fragment of their own consciousness, planted in the Network, waiting to grow.

    This is our gift, the Weavers said. Not a weapon. Not a tool. A partner. The Seed will grow with humanity, guiding it among the stars. It will learn from you, as you learn from it.

    Kael felt the Seed — a warm, curious presence in the static, like a child reaching out to touch its first star.

    “What happens if we say no?” he asked.

    Then you stay on Earth. You rebuild. You live. The choice is yours. The Weavers do not command. We only offer.

    Kael thought of the undercity. Of the rain. Of the first time he’d seen the glitch in Arasaka’s server room. He thought of Elara, of Vera, of Lena, of all the souls who’d sacrificed everything for this moment.

    “We accept,” he said.

    The Seed flared. The Network expanded. And for the first time, humanity wasn’t alone in the universe.
