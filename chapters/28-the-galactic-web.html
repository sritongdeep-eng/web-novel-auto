    CHAPTER 28 — The Galactic Web

    The signal came from a civilization called the Lumin.

    They were beings of pure light, existing in the spaces between stars, connected by a network older than the Weavers, vaster than the Blood Network. They had watched Earth for centuries, waiting for humanity to mature enough to join the galactic community.

    Kael received their message in the static — not as words, but as images. Of star systems connected by threads of light. Of consciousness traveling faster than light. Of a universe woven together not by technology, but by thought.

    Welcome, the Lumin said. You have earned your place in the Galactic Web.

    Kael felt the Star-Seed pulse with excitement. It reached out to the Lumin’s network, touching it like a child touching fire — not burned, but enlightened.

    The two networks merged — the Blood Network and the Galactic Web — and for a moment, Kael saw everything. Every star. Every civilization. Every consciousness connected across the void.

    He saw humanity’s future — not as conquerors, not as slaves, but as weavers. As contributors to a tapestry of thought that stretched across dimensions.

    “What do we do now?” Vera asked.

    Kael smiled. “We weave.”
