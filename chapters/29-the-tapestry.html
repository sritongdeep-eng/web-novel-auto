    CHAPTER 29 — The Tapestry

    Humanity learned to weave.

    Not with looms or threads, but with consciousness. The merged Network — Blood Network plus Galactic Web — became a tapestry of thought, where every mind contributed its own pattern, its own color, its own song.

    Kael walked through the tapestry in the static, watching humanity grow. He saw poets compose verses that resonated across star systems. He saw scientists solve equations that had puzzled civilizations for millennia. He saw artists paint with light, creating ephemeral masterpieces that existed only in the Network.

    And he saw the Weavers and the Lumin working alongside humanity — not as teachers, but as partners. The experiment had evolved into a collaboration.

    “This is what Veridian was meant for,” Elara said, appearing beside him in the static. “Not just preserving humanity. Letting it grow.”

    Kael nodded. He felt the threads in his arm pulse with warmth — not with power, but with purpose. The Null Conduit’s watch had changed. He was no longer a guardian standing between humanity and destruction. He was a weaver, adding his own thread to the tapestry.

    “What’s next?” he asked.

    The tapestry shimmered, showing a new pattern — a universe yet uncharted, a consciousness yet unborn, a story yet unwritten.

    Everything, the Weavers whispered. Everything.
