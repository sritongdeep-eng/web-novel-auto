    CHAPTER 35 — The Next Chapter

    The next chapter wasn’t written in static or code.

    It was written in laughter — in children running through Kepler-186f’s lavender fields, their minds linked to the Network, their consciousness touching stars before they could walk. It was written in art — in painters who used light instead of pigment, whose masterpieces existed only in the tapestry, visible to every weaver across dimensions. It was written in science — in explorers who solved equations with collective thought, who built worlds from memory and imagination.

    Kael watched it all from the edge of the Loom, his threads still connected, still listening, still part of the pattern even if he no longer guided it.

    “Do you ever miss the old days?” Vera asked.

    Kael thought of the undercity rain. Of the noodle shop. Of the first time he’d jacked into Arasaka’s server and seen the glitch.

    “Sometimes,” he said. “But I don’t miss being alone.”

    Vera smiled. She took his hand, and together they stepped into the tapestry — not as guardians, not as bridges, but as weavers. As storytellers. As the first authors of a new age.
