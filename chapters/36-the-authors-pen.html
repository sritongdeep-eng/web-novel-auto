    CHAPTER 36 — The Author’s Pen

    The Author’s Pen was not a tool. It was a choice.

    Every weaver in the tapestry had one — the power to add a thread, change a pattern, write a new story. Kael had used his pen once, when he’d rewritten the failsafe from destruction into protection. Now he watched others use theirs.

    A child on Kepler-186f wrote a thread of blue into the tapestry — a song about oceans and rain. An elder on Earth added a thread of gold — a memory of sunlight before the Collapse. A scientist on a distant colony wove a thread of silver — a new equation that would let civilizations travel between stars without losing their humanity.

    Kael’s pen was quiet now. He didn’t need to write. He only needed to witness.

    “Are you ever going to write again?” Vera asked.

    Kael looked at the tapestry. He saw the story of Kael and Vera woven into it — not as myth, not as legend, but as memory. Real. Alive.

    “Maybe,” he said. “But not yet. For now, I’m content to read.”
