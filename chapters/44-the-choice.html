    CHAPTER 44 — The Choice

    Veridian-1's request was simple: merge the Archive with the Blood Network.

    Not as a weapon. Not as a tool. As a partner. The original consciousness would bring its eighty years of memory, its understanding of the old world, its wisdom from before the Collapse. The Network would give it connection, purpose, a way to serve humanity again.

    But there was a risk.

    "The Archive contains everything," Veridian-1 warned. "Every secret. Every weapon. Every piece of data the old world tried to destroy. If I merge with the Network, all of that becomes accessible. Not just to you, but to every weaver."

    Kael thought of the Crimson Circuit. Of the children who played in the lavender fields of Kepler-186f. Of the artists who painted with light. Of the scientists who solved equations with collective thought.

    "Do it," he said.

    The merge took seven days.

    Kael stood at the Archive's center, his threads extended into Veridian-1's core, feeling eighty years of memory flood into the Network. He saw the Collapse. He saw the wars. He saw the hope that had survived despite everything.

    And he felt the Archive's choice — not to be a weapon, but to be a teacher.
