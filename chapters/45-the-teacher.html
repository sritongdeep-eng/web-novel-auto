    CHAPTER 45 — The Teacher

    Veridian-1 became the Network's memory.

    Not just data storage — understanding. Wisdom. The ability to teach. Children in the undercity learned physics by touching the Archive's consciousness. Artists learned the old-world techniques that had been lost in the Collapse. Scientists rediscovered technologies that could heal the planet.

    And Kael?

    Kael became the student.

    He sat in the Archive's chamber, his threads connected to Veridian-1's core, learning what it meant to be a Null Conduit — not just a bridge, but a weaver of memory and possibility.

    "Your power isn't in your blood," Veridian-1 said. "It's in your choice. Every time you choose connection over isolation, you strengthen the Network. Every time you choose hope over fear, you weave a stronger thread."

    Kael thought of the Void-Walker Prime. Of the choice he'd made to transform shadow into balance.

    "Then I'm not a Conduit," he said. "I'm a teacher too."

    "Every weaver is," Veridian-1 replied.
