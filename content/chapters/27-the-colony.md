# CHAPTER 27 — The Colony

The colony was called New Veridian.

Lena stood on its first hill, looking out over a landscape that was both alien and familiar. The planet — designated Kepler-186f by old-world astronomers — had oceans, forests, and a sky the color of lavender. The Blood Network stretched across it, a crimson web of consciousness connecting every colonist to Earth, to Kael, to the Star-Seed.

“It’s beautiful,” Jax said, joining her.

“It’s home,” Lena agreed.

The colony had grown fast. Within months, a thousand colonists had arrived through the Network — not physically, but consciously. Their minds projected through the static, inhabiting bodies grown from genetic templates, living on a world that had never known blood-tech slavery.

The Star-Seed pulsed around them, learning, growing, becoming. It touched the planet’s electromagnetic field and sang — a song of discovery, of connection, of life.

On Earth, Kael felt their wonder through the Network. He felt their joy, their fear, their hope. The bridge was working.

“They’re thriving,” Vera said, standing beside him in the control room.

“They are,” Kael agreed.

But even as he spoke, he felt something else — a signal from beyond Kepler-186f. From another star. Another world. Another civilization, connected to the Weavers’ network, reaching out to humanity for the first time.

The Star-Seed had been a seed indeed. And now, the garden was blooming.
