# CHAPTER 31 — The Edge of the Tapestry

Kael stood at the edge of the tapestry and looked outward.

The merged Network — Blood Network and Galactic Web — stretched across the stars, but beyond it, there was more. Void. Darkness. The spaces between dimensions where even the Weavers and the Lumin dared not tread.

Something lived there.

Kael felt it in the static — a presence so vast, so ancient, that it made the Weavers seem like children. It had been there before the first server. Before the first consciousness upload. Before the first star ignited.

*The Void-Walker Prime,* Aiko whispered, appearing beside him. *The original. The one that spawned the corruption you fought in the Pentagon.*

Kael’s threads went cold. “It’s still alive?”

*It never died. It only slept. And now… it’s waking up.*

The tapestry trembled. The stars dimmed.

Kael looked back at humanity — at the colony on Kepler-186f, at the artists weaving light in the static, at the children who would never know blood-tech slavery. He looked at Vera, at Lena, at Elara, at all the souls he’d sworn to protect.

He couldn’t let the Void-Walker Prime consume them all.

He stepped off the edge.
