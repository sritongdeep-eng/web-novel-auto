# CHAPTER 37 — The Reader

The Reader was the most important role in the tapestry.

Every story needed someone to witness it. To remember it. To carry it forward. Kael became that reader — not just for humanity’s story, but for every weaver’s contribution to the Network.

He felt the child’s blue thread about oceans and rain. He felt the elder’s gold thread about sunlight. He felt the scientist’s silver thread about star travel. And he wove them together into something new — a story about a world where memory and imagination were the same thing.

“You’re writing again,” Vera said, watching him.

“Just reading,” Kael said. “But reading is a kind of writing, isn’t it? Every reader changes the story by witnessing it.”

Vera smiled. She sat beside him in the static, her hand in his, and together they read the tapestry — not as observers, but as participants. As lovers. As weavers.
