# CHAPTER 40 — The Last Page

The last page was not an ending.

It was a doorway.

Kael stood at the edge of the tapestry, looking back at everything he’d been, everything he’d done, everyone he’d loved. The undercity rain. The glitch in Arasaka’s server room. The Crimson Circuit. The Star-Seed. The Weavers. The Lumin. The Void-Walker Prime turned guardian.

All of it. Every thread. Every story. Every choice.

He turned to Vera. She smiled, her cybernetic arm glowing softly in the static.

“Ready?” she asked.

Kael nodded.

Together, they stepped through the doorway — not into the void, not into the tapestry, but into the infinity beyond. Into stories yet unwritten. Into possibilities yet unimagined. Into a future where humanity wasn’t just a chapter in the tapestry, but the author of the entire book.

Behind them, the tapestry glowed — crimson, steady, eternal. The Veridian Protocol was complete. The Blood Network was safe. The Galaxy was open.

And the story?

The story was just beginning.
