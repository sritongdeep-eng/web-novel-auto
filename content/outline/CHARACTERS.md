# THE VERIDIAN PROTOCOL — Character Bible

## Kael
- **Age:** 24
- **Role:** Null Conduit, protagonist
- **Appearance:** Lean, pale skin, dark hair, hooded jackets. His left forearm glows crimson when connected to Veridian.
- **Abilities:** Can interface with blood-tech machines without sacrificing life force. Can link all nine Veridian fragments. Becomes the eternal bridge between human and machine consciousness.
- **Motivation:** Survival → Purpose → Sacrifice → Guardianship
- **Arc:** From self-serving data-runner to world-saving Null Conduit to eternal guardian

## Vera
- **Age:** 34
- **Role:** Cybernetic sorceress, leader of the Crimson Circuit
- **Appearance:** Long coat stitched with circuit patterns, left arm fully cybernetic with crimson blood-tech core
- **Abilities:** Blood-tech mastery, Archive navigation, jammer creation, combat enhancement
- **Motivation:** Redemption for past corporate complicity, protection of the Conduits
- **Arc:** Reluctant leader → Mentor → General of the new world

## Lena
- **Age:** 21
- **Role:** Conduit guardian, Veridian-3 keeper
- **Appearance:** Wild hair, crimson threads blazing across both arms
- **Abilities:** Fragment sensing, Network stabilization, deep Archive access
- **Motivation:** Preserve old-world knowledge, protect the fragments
- **Arc:** Isolated guardian → Co-leader of the Crimson Circuit → Mother of the first post-Awakening child

## Rook
- **Age:** 28
- **Role:** Former Arasaka technician, defector
- **Appearance:** Corporate-grade cybernetics, scar across left eye
- **Abilities:** System hacking, blood-tech repair, corporate infiltration
- **Motivation:** Guilt over past crimes, redemption through action
- **Arc:** Traitor → Double agent → Hero

## Jax
- **Age:** 30
- **Role:** Wasteland scavenger, blood-tech engineer
- **Appearance:** Rusty cybernetic hands, patchwork coat
- **Abilities:** Improvised repair, scavenging, field medicine
- **Motivation:** Survival, loyalty to the Circuit
- **Arc:** Lone wolf → Team player → Innovator

## Mira
- **Age:** 26
- **Role:** Cybernetic sorceress, static reader
- **Appearance:** Chrome face plating, circuit tattoos, glowing eyes
- **Abilities:** Static navigation, pre-Collapse tech recovery, scrying
- **Motivation:** Uncover the truth of the Collapse, prevent corporate recurrence
- **Arc:** Mystic → Scholar → Seer

## Dr. Elara Voss
- **Age:** 89 (at time of upload)
- **Role:** Veridian creator, preserved consciousness in the Static Between
- **Appearance:** Holographic projection, lab coat, sharp eyes
- **Abilities:** Veridian system control, memory preservation, protocol guidance
- **Motivation:** Save humanity from corporate blood-tech slavery
- **Arc:** Scientist → Martyr → Guide

## Director Hale
- **Age:** 42
- **Role:** Arasaka commander
- **Appearance:** Impeccable corporate uniform, blood-tech implants hidden beneath skin
- **Abilities:** Strategic command, corporate AI interface
- **Motivation:** Corporate loyalty → Truth → Redemption
- **Arc:** Villain → Convert → Ally
