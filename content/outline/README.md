# THE VERIDIAN PROTOCOL — Series Outline

**Genre:** Sci-Fi / Dark Fantasy  
**Setting:** Earth, 2187. The Collapse destroyed digital infrastructure, but ancient technology merged with arcane blood magic. Society rebuilt on "blood-tech" — circuits powered by human life force.  
**Protagonist:** Kael, 24, a data-runner in the undercity. He discovers he can interface with machines through blood magic without sacrificing his own vitality.

## Chapter Arc

1. **"The Glitch in the Blood"** — Kael discovers his unique ability during a routine data heist from a corporate server.
2. **"The Crimson Circuit"** — He meets a secret society of blood-tech practitioners who reveal he's a "Null Conduit."
3. **"Fragments of the Old World"** — Kael uncovers ancient server relics that predate the Collapse, containing AI echoes.
4. **"The Witch of Sector 7"** — Alliance with a cybernetic sorceress who teaches him to read the static between worlds.
5. **"Protocol Zero"** — The megacorporation discovers his ability and marks him for extraction.
6. **"The Blood Network"** — Kael connects with other Null Conduits across the wastelands.
7. **"Echoes in the Static"** — Pre-Collapse AI fragments reveal the truth about the Veridian Protocol.
8. **"The Veridian Core"** — The ancient AI system that can either restore Earth or end all blood-tech.
9. **"Sacrifice Protocol"** — Kael must merge his consciousness with Veridian to prevent cataclysm.
10. **"New Genesis"** — The world reborn. Kael becomes the eternal guardian of the Crimson Circuit.
